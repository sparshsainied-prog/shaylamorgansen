The Elm Stone Saga

Chosen

Sometimes we choose our way. Other times it is chosen for us.

The White Elm council has governed the magical societies of the world for centuries. When powerful Lisandro announces he is leaving, the Elm Stone, a ring holding years of the council's power, is taken by its keeper to his new group of malicious magic users.

Young Irish sorceress Aristea Byrne competes for a place as an apprentice to the White Elm, and starts to wonder how much is still within her control and how much has been chosen for her.

A prequel tale

All proceeds to WIRES.

Burned is a ten thousand word short story that sits three years before Chosen. When Emmanuelle and Peter are finally sent on their first assignment together, it is to help with crisis relief for a bushfire raging in Australia. She begins to find her voice and purpose, and Fate's.

Her book page says all proceeds of the ebook will be donated to WIRES (Wildlife Information Rescue and Education Service) and their work rescuing and rehabilitating wildlife affected by the Australian bushfires.

It contains references to animals hurt in bushfire conditions, which may be disturbing to some younger readers.

Mythos Unbound

Would you know a monster if you saw one?

Finn Bryant lectures in mythology by day and moonlights as a supernatural hunter, tracking the glamoured Shadow Kin he alone sees walking among us. His sister Evie profiles serial killers for the FBI and does not see the supernatural at all.

When a series of brutal murders with a mythological overtone has Evie consulting with Finn, the siblings find themselves hunting the same killer. But whose monster is it?

An adult urban fantasy with horror and forensic elements. Minimal romance, moderate stabbiness, maximum story.

About

Magical happenings in ordinary places.

Shayla writes lengthy books about the supernatural crossing paths with the everyday. Her favourite themes are interdependency, found families, us against the world, and a good helping of angst.

  • HomeBrisbane, with her husband, daughter and two naughty white cats.
  • Before the booksSchoolteacher, editor, assistant publisher and lecturer.
  • FandomsThe X-Files, Dexter, Stargate SG-1, Star Wars, Supernatural, Fringe, Sherlock and Farscape.

There's a little magic in all of us.
